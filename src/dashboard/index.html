Roster slots

Supported: DraftSheets .xlsx, or the ECR tab exported as CSV, plus schemaVersion 1 JSON. DraftSheets imports use the top 500 players by overall rank.

CSV/JSON may add all six optional Breakout Watch fields: source, as-of date, projected points, projected opportunities, opportunity kind, and experience years. Use touches for RB; targets or receptions for WR/TE. DraftSheets .xlsx does not import this evidence. When configured, fresh FantasyPros projections can also be combined automatically with conservatively matched, locally cached Sleeper experience.

CSV and JSON are reduced to allowlisted ranking fields in your browser. XLSX bytes go only to the bounded loopback parser and are never stored; filenames, URLs, notes, and arbitrary cells are not persisted.
